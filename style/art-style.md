# Art style

Attach this text and the approved anchor images to every panel prompt.

## Style prompt

> Hand-drawn storybook cartoon. Clean dark ink linework with light hatching, flat muted colors with a soft watercolor wash, warm cream/off-white background, minimal shading. Simple expressive faces (small dot eyes, few lines), slightly exaggerated proportions. Not photorealistic, not anime, not glossy 3D. Calm, legible composition with one clear focal action.

## Recognizability rule

Characters are recognized by their **signature look**: hair, costume, props, silhouette and setting. They are not portraits of the actors. Every character sheet lists those signature traits, and every panel prompt repeats them.

## Format

- Long-form panels: landscape (3:2 or 16:9), with spare room around the subject so the pan and zoom has space to move.
- Shorts: crop from landscape, or generate in portrait when the shot needs it.
- No text inside images. Titles and captions are added during assembly.

## Anchor images

Approved example scenes that define the style live in `style/anchors/`. Add 2 or 3 before generating a show.
